REAL ESTATE

The aim of this project is to build a comprehensive Real Estate Market intelligence solution that enables stakeholders to monitor market health, analyze demand - supply dynamics & identify high ROI investment opportunities using data - driven.

1) Executive Market Overview Dashboard

 To provide a high - level snapshot of the overall real estate market performance so that executive can quickly asses market health, & sales momentum at a glance.


2) Demand–Supply & Pricing Intelligence 

To analyze supply - demand gaps & pricing behavior across the cities & areas in order to identify market imbalances, inventory pressure & buyer vs seller market condition.


3) Investment & ROI Analysis

To evaluate the Real Estate investment opportunities by comparing excepted returns, rental yield%, price appreciations & risk levels, enabling by data - driven Buy, Hold or Avoid decisions.
